# Backlog 1: Target/Budget Data

Ngay ghi nhan: 11/08/2026
Trang thai: backlog, chua trien khai

## Muc tieu

Bo sung du lieu ke hoach de co the phan tich `actual` so voi `target`.

## Pham vi du kien

- `sales_targets`: muc tieu doanh so theo thoi gian, chi nhanh, kenh ban hang hoac san pham.
- `quantity_targets`: muc tieu san luong neu can phan tich them so luong.
- Bang phan bo muc tieu theo cap to chuc hoac ky kinh doanh neu can.

## Can lam ro truoc khi trien khai

- Grain cua tung bang target.
- Ky muc tieu: ngay, thang, quy hoac nam.
- Don vi do: doanh thu, so luong, don hang hoac bien loi nhuan.
- Phien ban ke hoach: original plan, revised plan, forecast.
- Trang thai hieu luc cua target.

## Ket qua mong muon

Co the tao bang phan tich so sanh doanh thu/san luong thuc te voi ke hoach va tinh ty le hoan thanh.
