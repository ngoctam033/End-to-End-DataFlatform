# Backlog 5: Expand Transaction Volume

Ngay ghi nhan: 11/08/2026
Trang thai: backlog, chua trien khai

## Muc tieu

Mo rong so luong don hang va giao dich de phuc vu kiem thu pipeline voi du lieu lon hon.

## Pham vi du kien

- Sinh them nhieu don hang theo cac ngay khac nhau.
- Mo rong so luong order lines, invoices, payments, shipments va returns.
- Tao du lieu co tinh da dang theo kenh ban hang, chi nhanh, san pham va khach hang.

## Thoi diem phu hop

Chua can thuc hien o giai doan hien tai. Chi nen lam sau khi pipeline co cau truc on dinh hon va can kiem thu volume/performance.

## Ket qua mong muon

Co du lieu du lon de kiem thu incremental load, performance, aggregation va cac case phan tich theo thoi gian.
