# Backlog 10: Logging, Monitoring and Alerting

Ngay ghi nhan: 13/08/2026
Trang thai: backlog, chua trien khai

## Muc tieu

Theo doi suc khoe pipeline va canh bao khi co loi hoac du lieu cham cap nhat.

## Pham vi du kien

- Chuan hoa structured logs theo pipeline run va task run.
- Thu thap duration, throughput, row count va error count.
- Theo doi container, database va pipeline health.
- Canh bao khi pipeline fail, stale data, quality check fail hoac SLA bi vi pham.

## Ket qua mong muon

Co the nhanh chong xac dinh pipeline dang loi o dau va nhan canh bao phu hop.
