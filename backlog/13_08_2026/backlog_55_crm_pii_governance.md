# Backlog 55: CRM PII Governance and Access Control

## Muc tieu

Bao ve du lieu ca nhan CRM va kiem soat viec su dung customer data trong analytics.

## Pham vi

- Phan loai PII: email, phone, address va identity attributes.
- Masking/tokenization cho moi truong non-production.
- Phan quyen theo role/team va row-level access khi can.
- Retention, deletion va audit policy.
- Theo doi data access va lien ket voi lineage.

## Ket qua mong muon

CRM data duoc su dung dung muc dich, truy vet duoc va khong lo PII qua cac mart/bao cao.
